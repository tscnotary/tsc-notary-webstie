Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful information before your appointment.

These answers explain how mobile notarization and loan signing appointments generally work with TSC Notary.

Do you have a physical office?

TSC Notary is a mobile notary business. We travel to the agreed signing location rather than receiving clients at a storefront.

What identification should a signer bring?

Each signer should have a current, acceptable government-issued photo ID. Identification requirements can vary, so contact us before the appointment if you have questions about a particular ID.

Can you explain legal documents or tell me what to sign?

No. TSC Notary is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. Questions about legal meaning, document preparation or the effect of a document should be directed to the appropriate attorney, title company, lender or document provider.

Does submitting the request form confirm my appointment?

No. Your appointment is confirmed only after TSC Notary contacts you and verifies availability, location, document type and any other assignment requirements.

Do you provide service in Spanish?

Yes. English and Spanish service is available for qualifying appointments, including real estate signing appointments.

Are travel or after-hours fees possible?

Yes. Pricing can vary based on travel distance, appointment time, document package and assignment requirements. Any applicable fee will be discussed before confirmation.
